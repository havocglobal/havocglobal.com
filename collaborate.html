Our rules

18 or over

Everyone we partner with must be an adult. We don't work with under-18s as ambassadors or creators for any brand, including A.C.H Kidz.

Clear labelling

Paid, gifted and affiliate posts must be labelled as advertising, as the UK CAP Code and ASA guidance require. We will never ask you to hide it.

Children's safety

For A.C.H Kidz we work only with parent and family creators whose audiences are adults. We never ask children to promote anything.

FAQ

Collaboration questions

How quickly will you reply?

We reply to every application within 10 working days.

Do you pay collaborators?

Yes. Affiliates earn commission on every sale they send. Creators and Celebrity and Talent partners are paid a fee agreed in writing before any work starts.

Is there a minimum following?

Affiliates start at 5,000 followers on one platform. Presenters, athletes and public figures can apply at any size.

Can my agent or manager apply for me?

Yes. Choose “Talent (represented by agent or manager)” on the form and add their details. We will reply to them directly.

Do I have to label my posts?

Yes. Any paid, gifted or affiliate post must be clearly labelled as an ad, for example with #ad at the start. This is a UK advertising rule.
