        FAQ

        Questions

        What is Havoc Global?

        Havoc Global is a lifestyle: an experience lived through its brands. It is a London fashion house that owns and runs three clothing brands: Defiant Attitude (clothing for men and women), A.C.H Kidz (childrenswear for ages 5 to 12) and Hevicon (everyday wardrobe, coming soon).

        Who founded Havoc Global?

        Oliver Hevicon. He was born in Manchester, spent part of his childhood in Hong Kong around his father's work in the clothing trade, and started Havoc Global in London.

        Can I buy clothes on this website?

        No. Each brand sells on its own website: defiantattitude.com, achkidz.com and, soon, hevicon.co.uk. For orders, sizing and returns, please contact the brand you bought from.

        How can I collaborate with Havoc Global?

        Apply on our Collaborate page. We work with adults aged 18 or over, including celebrities, presenters, athletes and creators. Affiliates start at 5,000 followers; presenters and public figures can apply at any audience size.

        Where is Havoc Global based?

        London, United Kingdom.
