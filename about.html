About

A lifestyle, lived through our brands.

Havoc Global is an emerging British fashion house in London. We design and build distinct clothing brands, each with its own identity, audience and site.

Our story

Oliver Hevicon

Oliver Hevicon was born in Manchester. When he was a child, his family moved to Hong Kong, where his father worked as a supplier in the clothing trade: the mechanics of how clothes move from a sketch to a shelf.

The family later moved back to Manchester. The clothing trade stayed with him: something he understood, something he had grown up around, something he had always intended to return to.

Work later brought him to London, where he started Havoc Global: the structure behind a growing portfolio of distinct clothing brands, each one designed here, each one built to stand on its own.

        How we work

        Each brand stands on its own

        Defiant Attitude, A.C.H Kidz and Hevicon are separate brands with separate identities, audiences and sites. Havoc Global is the structure behind them, not a label on the clothes.

        Designed in London

        Everything we build starts here. London is where the work happens: the design, the decisions, the direction.

        We only claim what we can prove

        We do not use language we cannot stand behind. No inflated claims, no borrowed credibility.

        Children come first

        A.C.H Kidz is marketed to parents and carers, never to children. We don't share children's faces, schools or locations.

        People, not numbers

        We partner with people whose audiences genuinely suit a brand, whatever their size.
